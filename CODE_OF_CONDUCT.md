# Code of conduct

This project has one rule that matters: treat people decently.

Expected behavior:

- Be civil in issues, pull requests, and reviews. Assume the other person is
  trying to help.
- Criticize code, not the person who wrote it. Say what is wrong and why, and
  leave the author a way to fix it.
- Accept that people disagree about FreeBASIC semantics, and that the
  disagreement is often about a fact neither of us has checked. Point at
  `fbc` or the wiki instead of escalating.
- Take responsibility when you break something, and assume good faith when
  someone else does.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing a thread.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests
that cross these lines, and may ban a contributor who keeps crossing them.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer at `ebben.feagan@gmail.com`. Reports are
handled confidentially: the reporter's name is not posted anywhere unless they
want it to be. If a report concerns the maintainer, report it to GitHub through
the repository's "Report abuse" link on their profile instead.

Reporting a security vulnerability belongs in `SECURITY.md`, not here. That
channel exists so a fix can ship before the details are public.

The human maintainer is Ebben Feagan (`@mudhairless`). Most of the code in this
repository was written by an AI coding agent working under that account; see
`CONTRIBUTING.md`. The agent is bound by this policy like anyone else, and
reports about its behavior are read and acted on by the maintainer.
